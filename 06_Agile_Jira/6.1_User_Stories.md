# User Stories

## 1. Purpose

This document converts the approved business and functional requirements for the NovaCart AI Customer Support Chatbot into Agile user stories.

The stories are written from the perspective of the primary users of the solution and are intended to support backlog prioritization, development, UAT, and requirements traceability.

---

## 2. User Story Format

Each story follows the standard Agile format:

> **As a [user], I want [capability], so that [business value].**

Acceptance criteria will be defined separately using Given/When/Then scenarios where appropriate.

# 4. MVP User Stories

## EP-01 — Intent & Conversation

### US-001 — Identify Customer Intent

**As a customer,**

I want the chatbot to understand what I am asking,

**so that I receive the appropriate support without navigating multiple support options.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** FR-001

---

### US-002 — Clarify Customer Request

**As a customer,**

I want the chatbot to ask a clarification question when my request is unclear,

**so that I can provide the information needed to resolve my issue.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** FR-002

---

### US-003 — Collect Missing Information

**As a customer,**

I want the chatbot to ask for missing information,

**so that it can complete my request accurately.**

**Example:**

> Customer: "Where is my order?"

> Chatbot: "Please provide your order number."

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** FR-002

---

# EP-02 — Self-Service Support

### US-004 — Check Order Status

**As a customer,**

I want to check my order status through the chatbot,

**so that I can get an update without contacting a support agent.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-004 / FR-003

---

### US-005 — Check Delivery ETA

**As a customer,**

I want to know the expected delivery date of my order,

**so that I understand when I should expect my package.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-004 / FR-003

---

### US-006 — Get Return Policy Information

**As a customer,**

I want to ask the chatbot about NovaCart's return policy,

**so that I can understand the applicable return rules without contacting an agent.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-003 / FR-004

---

### US-007 — Get Password Reset Guidance

**As a customer,**

I want to receive password-reset guidance through the chatbot,

**so that I can regain access to my account without contacting support.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** FR-004

---

### US-008 — Get Product Information

**As a customer,**

I want to ask questions about products,

**so that I can quickly find approved product information.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-003 / FR-004

---

# EP-03 — Customer Authentication

### US-009 — Authenticate Customer

**As a customer,**

I want my identity to be verified before account-specific information is provided,

**so that my personal information remains protected.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** NFR-002

---

### US-010 — Protect Customer Information

**As a customer,**

I want the chatbot to expose only the information required to answer my request,

**so that unnecessary personal information is not disclosed.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-005 / NFR-002

---

# EP-04 — Human Handoff

### US-011 — Request Human Agent

**As a customer,**

I want to request a human support agent,

**so that I can receive assistance when the chatbot cannot resolve my issue.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-005 / FR-005

---

### US-012 — Escalate Complex Issues

**As a support agent,**

I want complex or high-risk customer issues to be escalated to me,

**so that cases requiring human judgment are handled appropriately.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-005 / FR-005

---

### US-013 — Receive Conversation Context

**As a support agent,**

I want to receive the customer's chatbot conversation history and relevant context,

**so that I do not need to ask the customer to repeat information already provided.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** FR-006

---

# EP-05 — Knowledge & AI Guardrails

### US-014 — Use Approved Knowledge

**As a customer,**

I want chatbot answers about policies and FAQs to come from approved information,

**so that I receive reliable and consistent information.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-003 / FR-004

---

### US-015 — Prevent Unsupported Responses

**As a Product Manager,**

I want the chatbot to avoid providing unsupported information,

**so that customers are not given incorrect policies, prices, eligibility decisions, or other misleading information.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-003 / BR-006

---

### US-016 — Handle Low Confidence

**As a customer,**

I want the chatbot to acknowledge when it does not understand my request,

**so that I am guided toward clarification or human support instead of receiving an incorrect answer.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** FR-001 / FR-002 / FR-005

---

### US-017 — Handle System Failure

**As a customer,**

I want to receive an appropriate fallback when a required business system is unavailable,

**so that I am not given incorrect information.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** FR-007

---

# EP-06 — Analytics & Feedback

### US-018 — Capture Interaction Data

**As a Data/BI Analyst,**

I want chatbot interactions and outcomes to be captured,

**so that chatbot performance and business KPIs can be measured.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** FR-008 / BR-007

---

### US-019 — Capture Customer Feedback

**As a Product Manager,**

I want customers to provide feedback after chatbot interactions,

**so that customer satisfaction and improvement opportunities can be monitored.**

**Priority:** Should Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-008

---

### US-020 — Monitor Chatbot KPIs

**As a Support Manager,**

I want to monitor chatbot resolution, escalation, response time, CSAT, and deflection metrics,

**so that I can evaluate whether the chatbot is delivering the expected business outcomes.**

**Priority:** Must Have

**Related Requirement:** BR-010

---

### US-021 — Check Refund Status

**As a customer,**

I want to check the status of my refund,

**so that I do not need to contact a support agent for a routine update.**

**Priority:** Phase 2

---

### US-022 — Check Cancellation Eligibility

**As a customer,**

I want to know whether my order can be cancelled,

**so that I understand my available options before contacting support.**

**Priority:** Phase 2

---

### US-023 — Account Support

**As a customer,**

I want assistance with common account-related requests,

**so that I can resolve routine account issues through self-service.**

**Priority:** Phase 2

---

# 6. Human-Led Scenarios

The following scenarios remain human-led during the initial release:

- Complex complaints
- Payment issues requiring investigation
- High-value refund decisions
- Compensation requests
- Policy exceptions
- Complex fraud investigations
- Sensitive account investigations

The chatbot may collect basic information and route these cases to an appropriate support agent, but it should not independently make high-risk business decisions.

# 8. Definition of Done

A user story should not be considered complete until:

- Requirement is understood and approved.
- Acceptance criteria are agreed.
- Development is completed.
- Required integration/configuration is completed.
- Functional testing is completed.
- Relevant UAT test case is executed.
- Critical defects are resolved.
- Business stakeholder accepts the functionality.
- Traceability is updated.
- Story is ready for release.

---

# 9. Traceability Approach

Each user story should be traceable back to the approved requirements.

The project follows:

**Business Objective → Requirement → User Story → Acceptance Criteria → Test Case → UAT Result → Release**

This ensures that the Agile backlog remains connected to the original business need.
