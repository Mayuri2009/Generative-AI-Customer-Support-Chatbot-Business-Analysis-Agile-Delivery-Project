# Acceptance Criteria

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the acceptance criteria for the NovaCart AI Customer Support Chatbot user stories.

The criteria describe the expected system behavior from a business and user perspective and will be used as the basis for:

- Development validation
- Functional testing
- UAT test cases
- Defect identification
- Requirements traceability
- Business stakeholder acceptance

The criteria primarily use the **Given / When / Then** format.

> **Portfolio Note:** NovaCart is a fictional company and this is a simulated Business Analyst project.

---

# 2. Acceptance Criteria by User Story

## US-001 — Identify Customer Intent

**User Story:**  
As a customer, I want the chatbot to understand what I am asking, so that I receive the appropriate support.

### Acceptance Criteria

**AC-001-01**

- **Given** a customer enters a supported request
- **When** the chatbot processes the message
- **Then** the chatbot should identify the appropriate supported intent.

**AC-001-02**

- **Given** the customer asks about order status
- **When** the chatbot identifies the intent
- **Then** the intent should be classified as `Order Status`.

---

## US-002 — Clarify Customer Request

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-002-01**

- **Given** the chatbot cannot confidently determine the customer's intent
- **When** the customer message is processed
- **Then** the chatbot should ask a relevant clarification question.

**AC-002-02**

- **Given** the customer provides additional information
- **When** the chatbot processes the response
- **Then** the chatbot should attempt to identify the intent again.

**AC-002-03**

- **Given** the intent remains unclear after clarification
- **When** the customer still cannot provide sufficient information
- **Then** the chatbot should offer human support.

---

## US-003 — Collect Missing Information

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-003-01**

- **Given** a request requires additional information
- **When** the required information is missing
- **Then** the chatbot should ask the customer for the missing information.

**AC-003-02**

- **Given** the customer provides the requested information
- **When** the information is validated
- **Then** the chatbot should continue the relevant workflow.

**Example:**

Customer: "Where is my order?"

Chatbot: "Please provide your order number."

# EP-02 — Self-Service Support

## US-004 — Check Order Status

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-004-01**

- **Given** the customer provides a valid order number
- **When** the order status is requested
- **Then** the chatbot should retrieve the order status from the approved order system.

**AC-004-02**

- **Given** the order information is successfully retrieved
- **When** the chatbot responds
- **Then** it should provide the current order status.

**AC-004-03**

- **Given** the order cannot be found
- **When** the chatbot checks the order system
- **Then** it should not invent an order status and should provide an appropriate fallback.

---

## US-005 — Check Delivery ETA

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-005-01**

- **Given** the customer provides a valid order reference
- **When** delivery information is available
- **Then** the chatbot should provide the expected delivery information.

**AC-005-02**

- **Given** delivery information is unavailable
- **When** the chatbot attempts to retrieve it
- **Then** the chatbot should inform the customer and provide an appropriate fallback.

---

## US-006 — Get Return Policy Information

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-006-01**

- **Given** the customer asks about returns
- **When** the chatbot identifies the request
- **Then** it should retrieve the applicable information from the approved knowledge base.

**AC-006-02**

- **Given** the customer asks about a general return policy
- **When** approved policy information is available
- **Then** the chatbot should provide the relevant policy information.

**AC-006-03**

- **Given** the chatbot cannot find approved information
- **When** the return-policy request is processed
- **Then** it should not generate an unsupported policy answer.

---

## US-007 — Get Password Reset Guidance

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-007-01**

- **Given** the customer indicates that they have forgotten their password
- **When** the chatbot identifies the request
- **Then** it should provide the approved password-reset guidance.

**AC-007-02**

- **Given** the password-reset workflow requires authentication
- **When** authentication is required
- **Then** the chatbot should direct the customer through the approved verification process.

---

## US-008 — Get Product Information

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-008-01**

- **Given** the customer asks about a supported product
- **When** approved product information is available
- **Then** the chatbot should provide the relevant information.

**AC-008-02**

- **Given** the requested product information is unavailable
- **When** the chatbot searches the approved source
- **Then** it should state that the information is unavailable rather than inventing an answer.

---

# EP-03 — Customer Authentication

## US-009 — Authenticate Customer

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-009-01**

- **Given** the customer requests protected account-specific information
- **When** the request requires verification
- **Then** the chatbot should initiate the approved authentication process.

**AC-009-02**

- **Given** authentication is successful
- **When** the customer requests authorized information
- **Then** the chatbot may retrieve and display the permitted information.

**AC-009-03**

- **Given** authentication fails
- **When** protected information is requested
- **Then** the chatbot should not disclose the protected information.

---

## US-010 — Protect Customer Information

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-010-01**

- **Given** the customer requests information about their account
- **When** the information contains protected customer data
- **Then** the chatbot should expose only the minimum information required for the request.

**AC-010-02**

- **Given** the customer has not completed required verification
- **When** protected information is requested
- **Then** the chatbot should not disclose that information.

---

# EP-04 — Human Handoff

## US-011 — Request Human Agent

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-011-01**

- **Given** the customer asks to speak with a human agent
- **When** an agent-support option is available
- **Then** the chatbot should initiate the human handoff process.

**AC-011-02**

- **Given** the handoff is initiated
- **When** the case is transferred
- **Then** relevant conversation context should be made available to the agent.

---

## US-012 — Escalate Complex Issues

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-012-01**

- **Given** the customer presents a complex complaint
- **When** the chatbot determines that human judgment is required
- **Then** the chatbot should initiate human escalation.

**AC-012-02**

- **Given** the request involves a high-risk decision
- **When** the chatbot identifies the request
- **Then** the chatbot should not independently make the decision.

**AC-012-03**

- **Given** the case is escalated
- **When** the agent receives the case
- **Then** the agent should receive relevant conversation context.

---

## US-013 — Receive Conversation Context

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-013-01**

- **Given** a customer conversation is transferred to an agent
- **When** the agent opens the transferred case
- **Then** the relevant chatbot conversation history should be available.

**AC-013-02**

- **Given** the chatbot has identified an intent
- **When** the case is escalated
- **Then** the identified intent and escalation reason should be available to the agent.

---

# EP-05 — Knowledge & AI Guardrails

## US-014 — Use Approved Knowledge

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-014-01**

- **Given** the customer asks a supported FAQ or policy question
- **When** the chatbot generates a response
- **Then** the response should be based on an approved knowledge source.

**AC-014-02**

- **Given** the approved knowledge source contains updated information
- **When** the chatbot responds to the relevant request
- **Then** the current approved information should be used.

---

## US-015 — Prevent Unsupported Responses

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-015-01**

- **Given** the chatbot does not have sufficient approved information
- **When** the customer asks a question
- **Then** the chatbot should not invent an answer.

**AC-015-02**

- **Given** a customer asks the chatbot to make a policy exception
- **When** the request requires human authorization
- **Then** the chatbot should not independently approve the exception.

---

## US-016 — Handle Low Confidence

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-016-01**

- **Given** the chatbot has low confidence in its understanding
- **When** it processes the customer message
- **Then** it should ask a clarification question.

**AC-016-02**

- **Given** the chatbot remains unable to determine the customer's request
- **When** clarification does not resolve the issue
- **Then** the chatbot should offer human support.

---

## US-017 — Handle System Failure

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-017-01**

- **Given** a required business system is unavailable
- **When** the chatbot attempts to retrieve information
- **Then** it should not provide potentially incorrect information.

**AC-017-02**

- **Given** the required system remains unavailable
- **When** the customer needs assistance
- **Then** the chatbot should provide an appropriate fallback or human handoff.

---

# EP-06 — Analytics & Feedback

## US-018 — Capture Interaction Data

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-018-01**

- **Given** a customer starts a chatbot interaction
- **When** the interaction progresses
- **Then** relevant interaction information should be captured for analytics.

**AC-018-02**

The analytics data should support measurement of relevant metrics such as:

- Interaction volume
- Intent
- Resolution status
- Escalation
- Response time
- Customer feedback
- Fallback reason

---

## US-019 — Capture Customer Feedback

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-019-01**

- **Given** a chatbot interaction has been completed
- **When** the feedback option is presented
- **Then** the customer should be able to provide feedback.

**AC-019-02**

- **Given** the customer submits feedback
- **When** the feedback is recorded
- **Then** it should be available for performance analysis.

---

## US-020 — Monitor Chatbot KPIs

**Acceptance Criteria**

**AC-020-01**

- **Given** chatbot interaction data has been captured
- **When** performance reporting is generated
- **Then** relevant chatbot KPIs should be measurable.

KPIs include:

- Tier-1 query deflection
- Chatbot resolution rate
- Human escalation rate
- Response time
- Chatbot CSAT
- Knowledge-grounded response rate

# 3. Phase 2 Acceptance Criteria

The following acceptance criteria will be expanded when Phase 2 functionality is formally prioritized.

## US-021 — Refund Status

The chatbot should:

- Identify refund-status requests.
- Authenticate the customer when required.
- Retrieve authorized refund information.
- Provide the current status.
- Escalate when investigation is required.

---

## US-022 — Cancellation Eligibility

The chatbot should:

- Identify cancellation requests.
- Retrieve the relevant order information.
- Determine eligibility using approved business rules.
- Clearly communicate the available option.
- Escalate when an exception or human decision is required.

---

## US-023 — Account Support

The chatbot should:

- Identify supported account requests.
- Apply authentication where required.
- Provide approved self-service guidance.
- Escalate unsupported or sensitive account issues.

# 4. Cross-Functional Acceptance Criteria

The following criteria apply across the chatbot solution.

## Accuracy

The chatbot must not knowingly provide unsupported business information.

## Security

Protected customer information must only be provided after appropriate verification.

## Human Handoff

Customers must have a clear escalation path when self-service is insufficient.

## Auditability

Relevant interaction and outcome information should be available for analysis and monitoring.

## Reliability

System failures must result in controlled fallback behavior rather than unreliable responses.

## Usability

Customer-facing responses should be clear, concise, and easy to understand.

---

# 5. Definition of Acceptance

A user story can be accepted when:

1. All applicable acceptance criteria pass.
2. No critical or blocking defect remains open.
3. Required integrations behave as expected.
4. Security and data-protection criteria pass where applicable.
5. Relevant UAT scenarios pass.
6. The designated business stakeholder approves the functionality.
7. The requirement-to-test traceability is updated.

---

# 6. UAT Traceability

The acceptance criteria will be used to create the UAT test cases in Stage 7.

The intended traceability chain is:

**Requirement → User Story → Acceptance Criterion → UAT Test Case → UAT Result → Defect → Resolution → Release**

This ensures that the project demonstrates the complete Business Analyst lifecycle rather than treating Agile documentation as a standalone activity.
