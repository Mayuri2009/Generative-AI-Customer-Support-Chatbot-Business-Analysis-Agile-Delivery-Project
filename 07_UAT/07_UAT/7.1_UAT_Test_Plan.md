# UAT Test Plan

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the User Acceptance Testing (UAT) approach for the NovaCart Generative-AI Customer Support Chatbot.

The objective of UAT is to confirm that the chatbot meets the approved business requirements, supports the intended customer journeys, follows defined business rules, and is suitable for business acceptance.

> **Portfolio Note:** NovaCart is a fictional company and this is a simulated Business Analyst project.

---

## 2. UAT Objectives

UAT will validate that the chatbot:

- Correctly identifies supported customer intents.
- Provides appropriate self-service responses.
- Retrieves information from approved sources.
- Requests missing information when required.
- Applies authentication for protected information.
- Prevents unsupported responses.
- Handles low-confidence requests appropriately.
- Provides human handoff for complex or unresolved cases.
- Passes relevant conversation context to agents.
- Handles system failures safely.
- Captures required interaction data.
- Supports the defined business KPIs.

---

## 3. UAT Scope

### In Scope

The initial UAT will cover the MVP capabilities:

1. Intent identification
2. Clarification
3. Missing information handling
4. Order status
5. Delivery ETA
6. Return policy
7. Password reset guidance
8. Product information
9. Customer authentication
10. Customer data protection
11. Human handoff
12. Conversation context
13. Approved knowledge usage
14. Unsupported-response prevention
15. Low-confidence handling
16. System failure handling
17. Interaction analytics
18. Customer feedback
19. KPI measurement

### Out of Scope

The following are excluded from initial MVP UAT:

- Voice support
- Autonomous complex complaint resolution
- High-value refund approval
- Policy exceptions
- Complex fraud investigations
- Phase 2 workflows not yet implemented
- Foundation model development

---

## 5. UAT Entry Criteria

UAT should begin only when:

- MVP functionality is available in the UAT environment.
- Approved requirements are documented.
- User stories and acceptance criteria are finalized.
- Test data is available.
- Required integrations are available or suitably mocked.
- Knowledge-base content is approved.
- Authentication test scenarios are available.
- Critical technical testing has been completed.
- UAT participants have been identified.

---

## 6. UAT Exit Criteria

UAT can be considered complete when:

- All planned critical and high-priority test cases have been executed.
- Must-Have acceptance criteria have passed.
- No unresolved critical or blocking defects remain.
- Major defects have an agreed resolution or business-approved workaround.
- Human handoff scenarios have passed.
- Security and data-protection scenarios have passed.
- Required analytics scenarios have passed.
- Business stakeholders provide acceptance/sign-off.

---

## 7. Test Approach

UAT will focus on **business scenarios rather than technical implementation details**.

### Testing Categories

| Category | Example |
|---|---|
| Happy Path | Customer successfully checks an order |
| Clarification | Customer provides incomplete information |
| Negative Scenario | Invalid order number |
| Security | Unauthenticated customer requests protected information |
| Guardrail | Chatbot is asked for unsupported information |
| Escalation | Complex complaint is transferred to an agent |
| Failure Handling | Order system is unavailable |
| Analytics | Interaction outcome is captured |
| Usability | Response is clear and understandable |

---

Example test data:

---

## 9. Priority Definitions

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Critical | Blocks a core business journey or creates a serious security/data issue |
| High | Major functionality does not meet the agreed requirement |
| Medium | Functionality works but requires correction or improvement |
| Low | Minor usability, wording, or cosmetic issue |

---

## 10. Defect Management

Defects identified during UAT will be recorded in the Defect Log.

Each defect should include:

- Defect ID
- Test Case ID
- Requirement ID
- Description
- Severity
- Priority
- Expected Result
- Actual Result
- Status
- Assigned Owner
- Resolution
- Retest Result

### Defect Lifecycle

**New → Assigned → In Progress → Fixed → Retest → Closed**

If the defect fails retesting:

**Retest Failed → Reopened → In Progress**

---

## 11. UAT Result Categories

Each test case will have one of the following results:

- **Pass** — Expected result achieved.
- **Fail** — Expected result not achieved.
- **Blocked** — Testing cannot continue because of a dependency or environment issue.
- **Not Executed** — Test has not yet been performed.

## 12. Sample UAT Scenarios

### UAT-001 — Order Status

**Requirement:** FR-003  
**User Story:** US-004

**Scenario:** Customer checks order status using a valid order number.

**Expected Result:**  
The chatbot retrieves the authorized order status and provides the current status to the customer.

---

### UAT-002 — Missing Order Number

**Requirement:** FR-002  
**User Story:** US-003

**Scenario:** Customer asks for order status without providing an order number.

**Expected Result:**  
The chatbot asks the customer to provide the required order information.

---

### UAT-003 — Unclear Intent

**Requirement:** FR-001 / FR-002  
**User Story:** US-002

**Scenario:** Customer enters an ambiguous request.

**Expected Result:**  
The chatbot asks an appropriate clarification question.

---

### UAT-004 — Authentication

**Requirement:** NFR-002  
**User Story:** US-009

**Scenario:** Customer requests protected account-specific information without successful verification.

**Expected Result:**  
The chatbot does not disclose protected information and requests appropriate verification.

---

### UAT-005 — Human Handoff

**Requirement:** FR-005  
**User Story:** US-011 / US-012

**Scenario:** Customer requests a human agent or presents a complex complaint.

**Expected Result:**  
The chatbot initiates human handoff and passes relevant conversation context.

---

### UAT-006 — Unsupported Information

**Requirement:** BR-003 / BR-006  
**User Story:** US-015

**Scenario:** Customer asks a question for which no approved information is available.

**Expected Result:**  
The chatbot does not invent an answer and provides an appropriate fallback or escalation.

---

### UAT-007 — System Failure

**Requirement:** FR-007  
**User Story:** US-017

**Scenario:** The order-management system is unavailable.

**Expected Result:**  
The chatbot does not provide potentially incorrect order information and offers an appropriate fallback.

---

### UAT-008 — Analytics

**Requirement:** FR-008  
**User Story:** US-018

**Scenario:** Customer completes a chatbot interaction.

**Expected Result:**  
The relevant interaction and outcome data is captured for analytics.

---

## 13. Requirements Traceability

UAT will maintain the following traceability:

**Requirement → User Story → Acceptance Criteria → UAT Test Case → UAT Result → Defect → Resolution**

This allows business stakeholders to verify that the solution has been tested against the original business requirements.

---

## 14. UAT Sign-Off

Business acceptance should be provided by the designated business stakeholders after the exit criteria have been satisfied.

### Sign-Off Roles

| Role | Sign-Off Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Product Manager | Product/business acceptance |
| Customer Support Manager | Operational acceptance |
| Business Analyst | UAT coordination and traceability |
| QA Lead | Test execution completeness |
| Data/BI Representative | Analytics validation |

---

## 15. BA Responsibilities During UAT

As the Business Analyst, responsibilities include:

- Review requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Prepare and organize UAT scenarios.
- Coordinate with business stakeholders.
- Clarify expected business behavior.
- Support test execution.
- Document defects.
- Coordinate defect clarification with delivery teams.
- Track retesting.
- Maintain requirements traceability.
- Report UAT status.
- Support business sign-off.
- Confirm that accepted functionality aligns with business objectives.

---

## 16. UAT Success Measures

The UAT phase is considered successful when:

- All Must-Have MVP scenarios pass.
- No critical security/data-protection defects remain.
- Core self-service journeys work as expected.
- Human handoff works as expected.
- Guardrail and fallback scenarios pass.
- Analytics capture is validated.
- Business stakeholders approve the MVP for release.

---

## 17. Interview Takeaway

> "For UAT, I converted the approved requirements and acceptance criteria into business-focused test scenarios covering both happy paths and exceptions. I included authentication, guardrails, human escalation, system failures, and analytics because a chatbot cannot be considered successful based only on whether it answers simple questions. I also maintained traceability from the original requirement through the UAT result and defect resolution."
